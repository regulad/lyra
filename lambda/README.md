# Lyra

## ISP

Testing blurb:

To test the Lyra AI skill, begin by opening it for the first time and confirming that the user receives 10 free queries. Use these queries and verify that the skill prompts for a purchase after the 10th query. Check the query balance by asking Alexa to ask Lyra AI how many queries are left. Test query pack purchases by asking Lyra AI to buy more queries and explore the options: Small Pack (50 queries, $1.99), Medium Pack (150 queries, $4.99), and Large Pack (500 queries, $12.99). Purchase each pack using the Alexa in-skill purchasing flow and verify that the query count updates correctly. Test the monthly subscription ($9.99 per month for unlimited queries) similarly. After purchases, use the skill to ask questions and confirm that the query count decreases appropriately for packs or remains unlimited for subscriptions. Test the query limit by using all available queries and attempting to ask another question. For subscriptions, test cancellation through the Alexa app and verify that unlimited queries persist until the billing cycle ends. Finally, check that the skill suggests purchasing more queries when the user has 5 or fewer left. No special customer credentials are needed for testing, and all purchases can be made through the standard Alexa in-skill purchasing flow. Use a development version of the skill to avoid actual charges during testing.

### Detailed description

Lyra AI enhances your Alexa device with advanced artificial intelligence, transforming it into a knowledgeable companion
ready to answer a wide range of questions. This skill harnesses the power of cutting-edge AI technology to provide
quick, accurate, and insightful responses on topics spanning from history and science to current events and pop culture.

With Lyra AI, users can effortlessly access a vast knowledge base through simple voice commands. The AI system behind
Lyra is continuously updated, ensuring that the information provided is current and reliable. Whether you're a curious
learner, a trivia enthusiast, or someone who loves to stay informed, Lyra AI serves as your personal fountain of
knowledge.

Key Features:
• Extensive Knowledge Base: Covers a wide array of subjects
• User-Friendly Interaction: Just ask Alexa, and Lyra AI responds
• Up-to-Date Information: Regular AI system updates
• Flexible Usage: Includes free queries with affordable options for more

Lyra AI is designed to cater to various interests and information needs. It can assist with quick fact-checking, provide
in-depth explanations on complex topics, or offer engaging tidbits of information to satisfy your curiosity. The skill's
AI-powered responses aim to be not just informative, but also insightful, potentially offering new perspectives on
familiar subjects.

Users can start exploring Lyra AI's capabilities with the included free queries. For those who find themselves
frequently turning to Lyra for information, additional query options are available to extend the experience.

Unlock the full potential of your Alexa device with Lyra AI your gateway to AI-powered knowledge at your fingertips.

Lyra AI is powered by perplexity.ai.
